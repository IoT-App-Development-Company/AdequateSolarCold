# SolarCold controller assembly guide

This guide covers mechanical assembly of the Adequate SolarCold controller enclosure and installation of the monitoring PCB.

## Before assembly

- Verify PCB and enclosure revisions match.
- Use ESD protection while handling electronics.
- Disconnect every power source.
- Inspect printed parts for warping, cracks, and blocked mounting holes.
- Confirm cable glands and seals meet the deployment's environmental requirements.

## Assembly sequence

1. Install threaded inserts or standoffs without distorting the enclosure.
2. Seat the PCB with clearance beneath solder joints.
3. Route sensor, power, relay, and communications wiring separately where practical.
4. Add strain relief before terminating external cables.
5. Verify polarity, continuity, and isolation before installing fuses.
6. Close the enclosure without pinching cables or compressing sensors.
7. Apply power from a current-limited bench supply and complete the commissioning checklist.

Only qualified personnel should connect refrigeration controls, batteries, solar equipment, or mains-powered circuits.
