# Community code of conduct

Adequate Infosoft is committed to a professional, inclusive, and technically constructive project environment.

## Expected conduct

- Discuss ideas and evidence without personal attacks.
- Respect different experience levels, backgrounds, and working styles.
- Give actionable feedback and accept correction gracefully.
- Protect customer data, credentials, deployment details, and vulnerability reports.
- Credit upstream and third-party work according to its license.

## Unacceptable conduct

Harassment, discrimination, threats, deliberate disclosure of private information, license violations, and unsafe technical guidance are not accepted.

## Reporting

Report conduct concerns privately through Adequate Infosoft's established support channel. Security vulnerabilities should follow `SECURITY.md`. Reports will be reviewed with attention to safety, privacy, and fairness.

Project maintainers may edit or reject contributions, restrict participation, or remove community content when necessary to uphold these standards.
